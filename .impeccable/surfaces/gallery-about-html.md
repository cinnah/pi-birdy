# Gallery About page (birdy.akibeo.com/about.html)

Scope: one static page, no JavaScript. Visitor mode: Read. Audience: a friend who was sent the link, or a collaborator who wants to understand the thing they are about to put in their window. Job: understand what birdy is and why, how a visit becomes a row on the sheet, what the rig is, how to join, who to credit. Constraints: same world as the sheets (`gallery-index-html.md`); static files; self-hosted fonts; light and dark; never loud; no install tutorial, no contact details, no names, no repo URL (collaborators ask the owner directly).

## Direction

A typed note tucked into the record book: the sheet's masthead and head, then the sections as ruled form rows. On a wide sheet each section is a row of the form: the printed label (Libre Franklin, 11px tracked caps, rule ink) in the 230px margin column that the daily sheet uses for species, the entry typed in Courier Prime (15/1.65) in a 62ch column beside it. Under 1000px the label sits above its entry. Parts and credits are ruled definition lists; the pipeline is a typed flow in a 1px rule box (scrolls sideways on a phone). Red only on the double rules and focus. Links underlined in rule ink; the index line and the footer carry the 44px targets. The rig photo is a framed print at column width with a typed caption; until it is supplied a ruled 4:3 plate in paper tint holds the slot with a typed line. Nothing moves.
